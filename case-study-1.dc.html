Case Study 01 · Revenue Analytics

$70M Opportunity Identification

Role
Senior Strategic Operator
Context
Uber Freight · 165 accounts
Outcome
$70M identified

At Uber Freight, I owned the commercial relationship across 165 Fortune 500 accounts — a portfolio spanning retail technology, CPG logistics, and enterprise SaaS. The mandate was deceptively simple: find where the next wave of revenue would come from, and prove it with numbers the deal teams could act on.

I built the answer in data. Using advanced SQL against our shipment and pricing warehouse and layering Salesforce pipeline analytics on top, I modeled account-level economics — lane density, margin profiles, share-of-wallet, and expansion whitespace. Each account got a business model, not a guess. Those models translated raw customer insight into a ranked, defensible deal pipeline.

Three lessons

  1. 01Data is the new freight.
    The models moved as much value as the trucks did — insight, priced and prioritized, is inventory.
  2. 02Prioritization beats volume.
    A ranked pipeline of 40 accounts closed faster than a flat list of 165. Focus is the multiplier.
  3. 03Analytics must translate to sales action.
    A model no rep can use is a report. Every insight shipped with a next step attached.

The outcome: $70M in new revenue opportunities identified across the portfolio, with a qualified pipeline the sales organization could work in priority order — turning a decade of shipment data into a forward book of business.
