Case Study 02 · Commercial Negotiation

C-Level Partnership Expansion

Role
Senior Strategic Operator
Counterparts
CSCOs & CPOs
Outcome
−28% churn · +15% profit

The existing enterprise book was stagnating. Several of the largest accounts had plateaued on volume, renewal conversations were slipping, and churn had become a real threat to the portfolio's revenue base. Holding the line was not enough — these relationships needed to grow.

I led the high-stakes commercial negotiations directly with Chief Supply Chain Officers and Chief Procurement Officers. Rather than defend price, I structured complex, multi-year deals that addressed the full picture: tiered pricing, volume commitments, and geographic expansion into new regions. Each negotiation traded predictability for growth — the customer got certainty, we got committed volume and margin.

Three lessons

  1. 01C-level alignment requires mutual value.
    Executives don't buy discounts — they buy outcomes. Every ask was paired with something they needed.
  2. 02Expansion is harder than acquisition.
    Growing a scrutinized account means earning trust already spent. The bar is higher, and so is the payoff.
  3. 03Contracts are living documents.
    The signed deal was a starting point. Quarterly business reviews kept it expanding, not eroding.

The outcome: a 28% reduction in enterprise churn and 15% partner profitability growth — a stagnating book turned into an expanding one, account by account.
