Case Study 03 · Portfolio Strategy

Strategic Account Playbooks

Portfolio
165 accounts · $500M+
Approach
Segment & standardize
Outcome
100% playbook adoption

A $500M+ portfolio of 165 accounts does not behave as one thing. Some accounts needed white-glove executive attention; others were high-value but low-maintenance; many were growth bets that hadn't earned heavy investment yet. Treating them uniformly wasted the team's most scarce resource — senior attention.

I segmented the portfolio along two axes — strategic opportunity and account complexity — and built differentiated engagement strategies for each quadrant. Then I codified what worked into repeatable sales playbooks: cadences, stakeholder maps, expansion motions, and escalation paths. The goal was to make success a process, not a personality.

Three lessons

  1. 01Playbooks scale, relationships close.
    The playbook gets a rep to the table prepared; the relationship still wins the deal. Build both.
  2. 02Not all revenue is created equal.
    A dollar from a strategic account is worth more than a dollar from a transactional one. Segment accordingly.
  3. 03Process beats heroics.
    A portfolio can't run on individual brilliance. Standardized motions made every rep's best repeatable.

The outcome: optimized sales effectiveness and account growth across the entire portfolio — every one of the 165 accounts run on a playbook matched to its tier, with senior attention spent where it moved the most value.
