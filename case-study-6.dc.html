Case Study 06 · Commercial Strategy

International Consulting & Commercial Strategy

Firm
Guidepoint · GLG
Clients
15+ Fortune 500 · $500M+ spend
Outcome
4.9 / 5 satisfaction

At Guidepoint and GLG, I advise Fortune 500 companies — many with $500M+ in annual partnership spend — on enterprise commercial strategy. These are decisions made in the boardroom: where to concentrate capital, which partnerships to double down on, and which to unwind.

My work is to structure complex commercial analyses and build the business models that stand up to that scrutiny. I translate messy marketplace dynamics — pricing power, concentration risk, expansion economics — into clear, actionable recommendations for C-suite executives. The deliverable is not a slide; it is a defensible view on where the money should go.

Three lessons

  1. 01C-suite capital allocation requires rigorous financial modeling.
    A recommendation moving nine figures has to be right in the math before it's persuasive in the room.
  2. 02Consulting is about translating complexity into clarity.
    The value isn't the analysis — it's the one clear decision the client can defend afterward.
  3. 03Investment-grade recommendations build long-term trust.
    Get the high-stakes call right once and you become the firm's first call for the next one.

The outcome: investment-grade recommendations that influenced C-suite capital allocation decisions, delivered at 4.9/5 client satisfaction across 15+ Fortune 500 clients — a decade of operating experience, translated into advice boards can act on.
