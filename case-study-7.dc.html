Case Study 07 · Operational Excellence

24% On-Time Performance Gain

Role
Senior Customer Success Manager
Context
Uber Freight · Retail/CPG
Outcome
+24% on-time

Late loads erode trust faster than any pricing conversation. Across a high-priority portfolio of 165 Fortune 500 retail and CPG partners — including Target and Coca-Cola — on-time performance had become the metric that decided whether a relationship grew or quietly stalled. I owned the executive escalations, which meant I saw every failure mode before the dashboards did.

Rather than firefighting each miss, I implemented a root-cause analysis framework: every late shipment was coded to a driver — appointment adherence, lane capacity, tender timing, or facility dwell — and the codes were rolled up weekly. Patterns that had been invisible at the account level became obvious at the portfolio level, and the fixes moved upstream to where they scaled.

Three lessons

  1. 01Escalations are a data source.
    Every angry call was a labeled failure. Coding them turned complaints into a root-cause map.
  2. 02Fix the pattern, not the shipment.
    Solving one late load helps one account; solving its cause helps the whole portfolio.
  3. 03Reliability compounds into revenue.
    On-time gains rebuilt the trust that made every later expansion conversation possible.

The outcome: on-time performance improved 24% across the portfolio, converting the metric most likely to end a partnership into the one that anchored its growth.
