Case Study 05 · Brand Activation

Elite Brand Partnerships & Activation

Client
Jackson Hole Mountain Resort
Brands
Burton · K2 · Salomon · GoPro · Yeti · Jeep
Outcome
+25% seasonal revenue

At Jackson Hole Mountain Resort, I managed relationships with a portfolio of elite international brands — Burton, K2, Salomon, GoPro, Yeti, and Jeep. These were premium partners with premium expectations: their presence on the mountain had to feel as considered as their product.

I orchestrated the operational programs behind each partnership and served as the primary escalation point for time-sensitive partner needs — the person who made the season run. Beyond keeping the lights on, I developed and executed strategic activation programs: co-branded events, on-mountain experiences, and demo programs that turned a logo placement into a revenue engine.

Three lessons

  1. 01Brand alignment drives premium revenue.
    Partners paid for the right context, not just the audience. Fit was the product.
  2. 02Operational excellence is the backbone of partnership success.
    A flawless activation is invisible. The logistics no one noticed were the ones that renewed.
  3. 03High-touch escalation management preserves long-term relationships.
    Being the reliable point of contact under pressure is what turns a vendor into a partner.

The outcome: a 25% increase in seasonal program revenue — six elite brands renewed, every activation delivered on time, and a partnership program that ran like the mountain it was built on.
