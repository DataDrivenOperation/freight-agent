Case Study 08 · Product & Operations

17% Operational Efficiency Gain

Role
Senior Customer Success Manager
Context
Uber Freight · Eng & Product
Outcome
+17% efficiency · −28% churn

The best customer insight rarely lives in a survey — it lives in the friction customers hit every day and the workarounds they build to survive it. Sitting between 165 enterprise partners and the platform, I could see the same pain points recur across accounts long before they surfaced as churn risk. The question was how to turn that pattern into something the product organization could act on.

I partnered directly with Engineering and Product leadership, translating customer pain points and data trends into a prioritized feature case — quantified by the manual hours it would remove and the accounts it would retain. That case earned a place on the roadmap and shipped as a new platform feature, closing the loop between what customers felt and what the product did.

Three lessons

  1. 01Success sits closest to the truth.
    The account team hears the pain first. That vantage point is a product asset when it's routed well.
  2. 02Quantify to get on the roadmap.
    Hours saved and accounts retained beat anecdotes. A priced feature case wins the prioritization debate.
  3. 03Efficiency and retention move together.
    Removing friction cut churn as it lifted throughput — one intervention, two outcomes.

The outcome: the shipped feature increased operational efficiency 17% while reducing enterprise churn 28% and lifting partner profitability 15% — proof that a customer-success seat, used well, is a lever on the product itself.
